Four-Layer Architecture

Four layers of solution turning production complexity into an unmatched experience

01

Digital Immunity

Predictive Physics-Informed AI (PIAI) creates Hardware DNA — an immutable digital passport for each motor, recording micro-deviations from all 98 steps. Shadow Twin predicts failure of unique custom equipment weeks in advance, triggers 3D printing of rare components on-site.

Hardware DNA Shadow Twin PIAI
02

Phantom Haptic Signature

Micro-desynchronisation of inverter (Field Oriented Control) — imperceptible torque modulations mimicking "power waves" like cylinder firing. Adaptive Harmonic Generator (AGH) — piezo actuators amplify natural stator resonances, turning high-frequency whine into a structured electromagnetic roar.

Field Oriented Control Piezo Actuators Stator Resonances
03

The Aging Signature

First 1,000 km → sterile "Hollow Core" mode (max linearity). Neuromorphic coprocessor in inverter analyses driving style (pedal, steering, recuperation) over 10,000 km. System gradually adapts torque modulation and harmonic mix → evolving character that "matures" with the owner. On resale: new owner can reset to neutral or keep the "vintage" profile.

Neuromorphic Break-in Period Vintage Profile
04

Calibrated Uniqueness

Strict separation: Safety envelope (aerospace tolerances, ESP, brakes — invariant) vs Sensory non-linearity envelope (harmonic noise zone, no safety impact). Each buyer receives a "Digital Fingerprint of the Motor" document (7-10 micro-deviation parameters) with the statement: "This deviation lies within the spectrum of natural diversity and serves as the foundation of your personal character."
